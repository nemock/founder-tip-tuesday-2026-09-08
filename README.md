## This week

Jason Fried ran a free 37signals breakfast and 84 people said they were coming. 38 showed
up. So he ran the next one in Chicago with one change at the door: $100 charged at
registration, a crisp $100 bill handed back on the way in. 55 people paid, 50 came, and
the show-rate went from 45% to 90%. The $500 the no-shows left behind went to charity.

You can't charge a hospital $100 to hold a pilot slot. You can charge them time and hand
it straight back: a date with whoever owns the budget, the names of who else is in the
room, one page signed before a unit ships. None of that costs a site more than fifteen
minutes, and a site that won't spend the fifteen minutes is still just a conversation.
